Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. When you use the app, we store the expense records you create — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use trusted infrastructure providers to host and operate the service, who are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your data independently.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly and transparently.
